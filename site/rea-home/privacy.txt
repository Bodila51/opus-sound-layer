REA Home: what is counted

No cookies. Your IP address is not stored, and nothing is saved in your browser for this.
The photo is read on your device and never uploaded or shown. Brand, model and any other text read from a label are never sent.

One anonymous record per page visit, under a random id made for that visit only:
- rough location from Cloudflare: country, region, city
- device type (phone, tablet or computer), and the names of the browser and the operating system
- language, window width, dark or light theme
- the name of the site you came from
- time on the page, how long each part of the page was on screen, how far you scrolled
- which steps you reached: opened the camera, took or chose a photo, the label was read, a prompt was shown, a prompt was copied
- which devices you opened in the 3D home, the kind of device read (router, TV and so on), and how often the buttons were used
- how many page errors happened

The records are kept in a Cloudflare D1 database that only the site's owner can read.
